Simulator & Calculator Suite

Model Systems, Controllers, and Performance.

FTS Simulators allow teams to model the behavior of mechanisms to understand how design choices translate into performance. Practice tuning controllers to gain a better understanding of PID, and gain insight into potentially viable controller gains.

*NOTE: Simulations are only as useful as the quality of models and data that are input, use them as a guide to design, not a source of truth.
